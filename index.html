Our house

One house for your smile and your skin

Maison De Visage brings dentistry, orthodontics, dermatology, skin care and facial aesthetics together in one place. Our specialists work side by side, so your face is looked after as a whole.

Every visit starts with a consultation. We listen, examine, and explain your options clearly before any treatment begins.
